75 Broadway Residences

52 stories and only 106 homes across from the ten protected acres of Simpson Park, with open views to Biscayne Bay.

Developed by Ytech with architecture by Kohn Pedersen Fox, the tower brings together one-, two- and three-bedroom homes — three per floor — with private elevator foyers and expansive glass frontages.

Interiors are by ACPV Architects Antonio Citterio Patricia Viel, with Arclinea kitchens and Gaggenau appliances. Residential service has been developed with the British Butler Institute.
