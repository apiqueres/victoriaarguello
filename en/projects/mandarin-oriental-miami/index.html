The Residences at Mandarin Oriental, Miami

A 33-story tower above the bay that brings together private residences and the new Mandarin Oriental, Miami hotel.

Developed by Swire Properties on the last available parcel on Brickell Key, the project offers 70 private residences and 28 turnkey hotel residences, with direct access to Mandarin Oriental service.

Warm, light-filled interiors, private terraces and expansive windows open every room to panoramic views of the water and the skyline.
